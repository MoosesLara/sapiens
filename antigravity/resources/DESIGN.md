## Brand & Style

The design system projects an aura of transcendent leadership, spiritual wisdom, and elite business stewardship. Targeted at high-caliber executives, entrepreneurs, and visionary leaders seeking holistic wealth ("Riquezas, Honra y Vida"), the interface merges the private members' club aesthetic with high-ticket summit conversion rigor.

The visual direction combines **Old Money Elegance** with **Modern Sovereign Luxury**:
- An imposing, obsidian-carbon foundation creates an atmospheric backdrop that evokes reverence and exclusivity.
- Polished antique golds and warm metallic brass supply divine radiance, signaling wisdom and high status without superficial flashiness.
- High-contrast, clean typography pairs classical editorial authority with crisp, ultra-legible modern structures engineered specifically for high-impact mobile readability.
- The interface evokes solemn exclusivity, deliberate urgency, and unshakeable confidence.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an ultra-deep charcoal black, punctuated by radiant bullion gold hues derived from biblical metaphors of purified gold and wisdom.

- **Primary (`#C59B4E`)**: The signature sovereign gold. Used for critical conversion anchors, high-ticket badges, key headings, and active interactive borders.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37`)**: Luminous gold used for specular highlights, gradient apexes, icons, and live countdown numerical pulses.
- **Tertiary (`#B78A3A`)**: Deep antique bronze gold. Used for secondary actions, subtle pill strokes, and muted category headers.
- **Neutral (`#0D0D0E`)**: Midnight carbon base canvas ensuring deep contrast and an intimate, theater-grade mobile viewport.
- **Named Surfaces**:
  - `carbon-void` (`#070707`): Pure darkness for page foundations and fixed mobile navigation sheets.
  - `carbon-surface` (`#141416`): The standard container tone for pillar cards and countdown modules.
  - `carbon-elevated` (`#1B1B1F`): Stacked modal dialogs, drawer panels, and floating floating CTA containers.
  - `gold-light` (`#F2D07E`): High-luminance gold for subtle text accents and top-edge light glints.

## Typography

Typography establishes an aristocratic balance between sacred classicism and contemporary high-yield readability.

- **Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`)**: Expressive, high-contrast serif with razor-sharp serifs evoking fine publishing, biblical scholarship, and presidential prestige. Rendered with tight leading and authoritative grandeur.
- **Labels, Badges & Micro-headers (`Montserrat`)**: Formatted consistently in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.08em` to `0.16em`), evoking luxury heraldry and high-society clubs.
- **Body (`Inter`)**: Neutral, ultra-clear grotesk calibrated for maximum reading stamina against dark backgrounds, preventing eye strain while absorbing substantial didactic copy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive mobile-first architecture that prioritizes single-thumb ergonomics while maintaining elite ceremonial spacing.

- **Mobile Viewport (360px – 480px)**: 4-column layout with strict `1.25rem` screen margins (`margin-mobile`) and `0.75rem` gutters. Elements stack vertically into singular, high-focus modules to prevent visual clutter.
- **Sticky Conversion Architecture**: The primary conversion action rests within an elevated floating bottom bar with safe-area bottom padding, ensuring immediate actionability regardless of scroll depth.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Generous section spacing (`space-xl` to twice `space-xl`) isolates core narrative blocks—such as the three pillars (Riquezas, Honra, Vida) and the live countdown module—creating an intentional feeling of ceremony and gravity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on generic drop shadows, but on layered darkness combined with metallic rim lighting and radial gold atmospheric flares.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: Deepest void `#070707`. Non-interactive backdrop layered under subtle dark ambient vignettes.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation)**: `#141416` paired with a delicate, 1px border colored with `rgba(197, 155, 78, 0.18)` (`gold-border`).
- **Level 2 (Featured Pillars & Countdown Cards)**: `#1B1B1F` backed by an ambient gold aura: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(197, 155, 78, 0.12), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating CTA Dock & Modals)**: Frosted dark surface (`rgba(20, 20, 22, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), accented by a radiant top border gradient running from transparent through `#D4AF37` to transparent.

## Shapes

The design system employs an understated, tailored shape aesthetic (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Standard interactive controls and modular content cards utilize a subtle `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) radius. This restrained geometry communicates institutional tradition, precision tailoring, and private-bank permanence rather than casual playfulness.
- Exclusive badges and scarcity tags leverage a slightly tighter edge or a pure micro-beveled silhouette, reinforcing sovereign authority.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Sovereign Gold CTA ("Inscríbete Ahora")**:
  - Background: Linear gradient (`135deg, #F2D07E 0%, #C59B4E 50%, #8F6B27 100%`).
  - Text: Uppercase `Montserrat` Bold, 13px, colored `#070707` with `0.1em` letter spacing for immediate legibility.
  - Border: 1px top highlight `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`.
  - State: On press/tap, deepens in saturation with a brief inward glow.
- **Secondary Club Action ("Más Información" / "Agendar Llamada")**:
  - Background: Transparent with a 1px border in `#C59B4E`.
  - Text: `#C59B4E`, uppercase `Montserrat` SemiBold.

### Exclusivity Badges & Scarcity Seals
- Inline chips with dark carbon fill (`#141416`), bordered by 1px solid `#C59B4E44`.
- Accompanied by a pulsing amber/gold indicator dot signaling restricted capacity ("CUPO LIMITADO • EVENTO 100% PRESENCIAL").

### Pillar Cards (Riquezas, Honra, Vida)
- Built on `carbon-surface` (`#141416`) with an inset top hairline gradient highlighting the gold rim.
- Prominent Roman numeral or bespoke icon rendered in `#D4AF37` with subtle golden glow.
- Serif title in `Bodoni Moda` followed by concise, high-contrast value propositions in `Inter`.

### Event Countdown Module (Noviembre 2026)
- Horizontally segmented unit displaying Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds.
- Each time segment is housed in a beveled `#1B1B1F` tile featuring large tabular numerals in `Bodoni Moda` with a gold gradient mask and micro-labels in `Montserrat` uppercase.

### Testimonial Sliders & Authority Quotations
- Framed with subtle gold corner accents.
- Large editorial quotation marks rendered in semi-transparent antique gold (`rgba(197, 155, 78, 0.25)`).
- Card container features smooth touch gestures with gold pagination indicators (active: elongated gold bar; inactive: muted dark dot).

### Input Fields & Registration Forms
- Pure carbon backgrounds (`#0D0D0E`) with thin `rgba(197, 155, 78, 0.3)` borders.
- Focus state activates an illuminated `1px solid #D4AF37` border accompanied by an ambient `0 0 12px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)` glow.
- Typography: Label in `Montserrat` upper-case; input text in crisp `#F7F7F8` `Inter`.
